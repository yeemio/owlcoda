05 — PILOT SCOPEA DISCUSSION CHECKLIST. NOT A SIGN-UP FORM.

Start with one
well-defined piece of work.

Use these questions to frame a possible pilot with your team. No answers are entered, stored, or submitted on this page.

  1. The recurring work

    What task repeats, and where does understanding or judgment slow it down?

  2. Existing materials

    Which documents, records, and systems contain the facts? Who may access them?

  3. The useful output

    What document, comparison, or preparation would someone actually use?

  4. The reviewer

    Who checks the evidence, resolves exceptions, and makes the business decision?

  5. The evaluation basis

    Which examples, quality criteria, and failure cases would establish useful work?

  6. Actions that remain off-limits

    What must not be sent, changed, committed, or decided without explicit authority?
